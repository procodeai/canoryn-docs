# Your account

A Canoryn account lets you [publish documents](/guide/sharing-documents) to canoryn.app and carries your plan. Everything else in the app works without one. Since **0.6.1**.

## Signing in

Open **Settings › Account** and choose **Sign In** (or **Create Account**). Canoryn opens canoryn.app in your browser:

- If you are already signed in there, choose **Continue as** your account, or **Use another account**.
- If you chose Create Account while signed in to the site, you can create a new account or use the one you have.

Confirm, and the browser hands the Mac a one-time code. Canoryn exchanges it for a session kept in your Keychain; no password is stored in the app.

## The Account page

- **You** — your name and email, and **Manage on canoryn.app**.
- **Plan** — your plan with a **Verified** tag. The app trusts a plan only from a signed entitlement; the tag shows whether it is verified, being checked, or not verified, and ↻ checks again.
- **This Mac** — the name this Mac shows under your account, and a link to manage your devices. A Mac removed on the website stays removed.

## Signing out

**Sign Out** on the Account page (or in the sidebar's account menu) ends the session on this Mac. Your documents and chats stay on the Mac; published pages stay online until you unpublish them.

## More than one account

Signing in with a second account keeps both. Only then does the account menu show the other account to switch to. A published page remembers which account published it, and the Share panel offers to switch if it was another one.
